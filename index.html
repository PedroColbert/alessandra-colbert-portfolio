01 — ABOUT ME

About Me

Passionate bilingual educator with a degree in Languages, currently pursuing a second degree in Pedagogy, and undergoing postgraduate studies in both Bilingual and Early Childhood Education. With experience leading immersion classrooms in prominent bilingual schools, my work focuses on building joyful, academically rich environments where multilingualism develops organically.

THE APPROACH

Teaching Philosophy

I view children as naturally curious, competent researchers of their environment. My pedagogical practice is grounded in:

01

Inquiry-Based Learning

Honoring children's questions as the starting point for deep exploration.

02

Language as a Living Tool

Creating authentic immersion contexts where English and Portuguese are vehicles for thought, expression, and discovery.

03

Process over Product

Valuing creative agency, critical thinking, and socio-affective growth through hands-on experiences.

SELECTED PROJECTS01 / 05

PEDAGOGICAL PROJECT · 01

Hands-On Autonomy & Fine Motor Mastery

Recognizing children’s natural desire for independence in daily self-care routines, this practical project transformed the challenge of tying shoelaces into an engaging, multi-sensory challenge. Using a tactile manipulative crafted from repurposed PVC pipes and colorful laces, students explored knotting mechanics in a scaled, accessible format before applying the skill to their own shoes.

SELECTED PROJECTS02 / 05

PEDAGOGICAL PROJECT · 02

Strategic Minds & Early Numeracy:
Fostering Logical Reasoning Through Game-Based Learning

Transforming abstract mathematical concepts into engaging, social experiences through game-based inquiry. By pairing the strategic foresight of chess with tactile dice-addition challenges, students were invited to investigate spatial relationships, patterns, and arithmetic operations in an authentic, low-stakes setting that celebrates playful problem-solving.

SELECTED PROJECTS03 / 05

PEDAGOGICAL PROJECT · 03

Little Makers, Big Ideas: Transforming Recycled Caps into Playful Prototypes

Bridging abstract imagination, intentional 2D planning, and 3D physical construction through a child-led Design Thinking cycle. Guided by questions of environmental responsibility and inventive play, students took on the role of toy inventors: first drafting initial sketches on paper and then bringing their concepts to life using upcycled plastic bottle caps.

SELECTED PROJECTS04 / 05

PEDAGOGICAL PROJECT · 04

Trees in Texture & Tone Using Watercolor

Merging botanical observation with material inquiry and foundational motor development. Inspired by the varied bark textures, branches, and foliage of trees on the school grounds, students investigated the physical dialogue between water, paper, and pigment. Beyond exploring fluid watercolors, this project was deeply instrumental in refining the dynamic tripod brush grip, modulating delicate hand pressure, and cultivating emotional patience, guiding children to discover that translating nature's organic textures into visual art requires mindful pacing, deliberate physical control, and sustained attention.

SELECTED PROJECTS05 / 05

PEDAGOGICAL PROJECT · 05

Daily Sound to Script: Systematic Phonics, Phoneme-Grapheme Mapping & Whiteboard Dictation

Establishing a predictable, high-engagement daily literacy ritual centered on explicit phonics and active phoneme-to-grapheme encoding. Moving away from passive worksheets, students gather daily around the whiteboard to segment spoken words into individual acoustic sounds and immediately transcribe them into written symbols. Utilizing the whiteboard as a shared, large-scale, and erasable surface lowers the affective filter, inviting children to take risks with emergent spelling, celebrate approximations, and experience the joy of breaking the alphabetic code in real time.
